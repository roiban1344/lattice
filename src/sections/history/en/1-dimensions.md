---
title: Dimension by dimension
---

This page lists the extremal even unimodular lattices known today, dimension by dimension, together with who found them and when. How they were constructed is the subject of the [next page](../constructions/).

Up to dimension $24$ the even unimodular lattices are completely classified, so the extremal ones are known exactly: $E_8$ in dimension $8$, the two lattices $E_8\perp E_8$ and $D_{16}^{+}$ in dimension $16$ (the bound is only $2$ there, so both are extremal), and the Leech lattice in dimension $24$. In dimensions $32$ and $40$ extremal lattices are so numerous that they are counted rather than listed. From dimension $48$ on, each example is the result of a specific construction, and the table lists them individually.
