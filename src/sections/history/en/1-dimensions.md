---
title: Dimension by dimension
---

This page lists the extremal even unimodular lattices known today, dimension by dimension, together with who found them and when. How they were constructed is the subject of the [next page](../constructions/).

Up to dimension $24$ the even unimodular lattices are completely classified, so all the extremal ones are known. They are $E_8$ in dimension $8$, the two lattices $E_8\perp E_8$ and $D_{16}^{+}$ in dimension $16$ (the bound is only $2$ there, so both are extremal), and the Leech lattice in dimension $24$.

In dimensions $32$ and $40$ there are so many extremal lattices that they cannot be listed; only lower bounds for their number are known. From dimension $48$ on, every known example comes from an explicit construction. The table names individual lattices where only a few are known.
