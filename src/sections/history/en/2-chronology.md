---
title: Chronology
---

The list below gives, in order of publication, the constructions of extremal lattices in dimensions $24$ and above, together with two results on the bound itself. Years are those of publication; where a preprint appeared earlier, both years are given.

- **1967.** Leech publishes the Leech lattice $\Lambda_{24}$ [[Lee67]](#ref-Lee67). Two years later Conway proves that it is the only even unimodular lattice of dimension $24$ without vectors of norm $2$ [[Con69]](#ref-Con69).
- **1971.** Leech and Sloane construct the first two extremal lattices of dimension $48$, $P_{48p}$ and $P_{48q}$, from ternary codes [[LS71]](#ref-LS71).
- **1975.** Mallows, Odlyzko and Sloane prove the bound $\min\Lambda\le 2\lfloor n/24\rfloor+2$ and show that extremal lattices cannot exist in large dimensions [[MOS75]](#ref-MOS75).
- **1984.** Quebbemann introduces a general construction and obtains an extremal lattice of dimension $64$ [[Que84]](#ref-Que84).
- **1989.** Ozeki obtains an extremal lattice of dimension $56$ from ternary codes [[Oze89]](#ref-Oze89).
- **1995.** Batut, Quebbemann and Scharlau find another one in dimension $56$, using cyclotomic fields [[BQS95]](#ref-BQS95).
- **1998.** Nebe constructs a third extremal lattice of dimension $48$, $P_{48n}$, and lattices of dimension $56$ with the symmetry group $\mathrm{SL}_2(29)$ [[Neb98]](#ref-Neb98). In the same year Bachoc and Nebe construct two extremal lattices of dimension $80$ [[BN98]](#ref-BN98).
- **2010.** Stehlé and Watkins prove that a third lattice of dimension $80$ is extremal [[SW10]](#ref-SW10). Nebe constructs $\Gamma_{72}$, the first extremal lattice of dimension $72$ (preprint 2010, published 2012) [[Neb12]](#ref-Neb12).
- **2011.** Jenkins and Rouse show that extremal lattices do not exist in any dimension greater than $163\,264$ [[JR11]](#ref-JR11).
- **2012.** Watkins finds a fourth extremal lattice of dimension $80$ [[Wat12]](#ref-Wat12).
- **2014.** Nebe finds a fourth extremal lattice of dimension $48$, $P_{48m}$ [[Neb14]](#ref-Neb14).
- **2018, 2022.** Shimada gives a new construction in dimension $64$ [[Shi18]](#ref-Shi18) and shows that Quebbemann's construction produces many extremal lattices of dimension $64$ [[Shi22]](#ref-Shi22).
- **2026.** Sakamoto constructs two extremal lattices of dimension $88$ (preprint) [[Sak26]](#ref-Sak26). In October, an extremal lattice of dimension $112$, $L_{112}$, is announced with a computer-assisted proof (preprint) [[L112]](#ref-L112); the [page on $L_{112}$](../l112/) describes it.
