---
title: Chronology
---

The list below gives the constructions of extremal lattices in dimensions $24$ and above, together with two results that limit the dimensions in which extremal lattices can exist. Each item is placed in the year in which the result first became public, either as a **preprint** (a paper made public before peer review) or in a journal.

- **1967.** Leech publishes the Leech lattice $\Lambda_{24}$ [[Lee67]](#ref-Lee67). Two years later Conway proves that it is the only even unimodular lattice of dimension $24$ without vectors of norm $2$ [[Con69]](#ref-Con69).
- **1971.** Leech and Sloane construct the first two extremal lattices of dimension $48$, $P_{48p}$ and $P_{48q}$, from ternary self-dual codes (codes over the field with three elements; see the [next page](../constructions/)) [[LS71]](#ref-LS71).
- **1975.** Mallows, Odlyzko and Sloane prove the bound $\min\Lambda\le 2\lfloor n/24\rfloor+2$ and show that extremal lattices cannot exist in sufficiently large dimensions [[MOS75]](#ref-MOS75).
- **1984.** Quebbemann introduces a general construction and obtains an extremal lattice of dimension $64$ [[Que84]](#ref-Que84).
- **1989.** Ozeki obtains an extremal lattice of dimension $56$ from ternary codes [[Oze89]](#ref-Oze89).
- **1995.** Batut, Quebbemann and Scharlau find another extremal lattice of dimension $56$, using cyclotomic fields, the fields obtained by adjoining roots of unity to $\mathbb{Q}$ [[BQS95]](#ref-BQS95).
- **1998.** Nebe constructs a third extremal lattice of dimension $48$, $P_{48n}$. The same paper also constructs extremal lattices of dimension $56$ on which the group $\mathrm{SL}_2(29)$ acts [[Neb98]](#ref-Neb98). In the same year Bachoc and Nebe construct two extremal lattices of dimension $80$ [[BN98]](#ref-BN98).
- **2010.** Stehlé and Watkins prove that a third lattice of dimension $80$ is extremal [[SW10]](#ref-SW10). Nebe constructs $\Gamma_{72}$, the first extremal lattice of dimension $72$ (preprint 2010, published 2012) [[Neb12]](#ref-Neb12).
- **2011.** Jenkins and Rouse show that extremal lattices do not exist in any dimension greater than $163\,264$ [[JR11]](#ref-JR11).
- **2012.** Watkins finds a fourth extremal lattice of dimension $80$ [[Wat12]](#ref-Wat12).
- **2013.** Nebe finds a fourth extremal lattice of dimension $48$, $P_{48m}$ (preprint 2013, published 2014) [[Neb14]](#ref-Neb14).
- **2018, 2022.** Shimada gives a new construction in dimension $64$ [[Shi18]](#ref-Shi18) and shows that Quebbemann's construction produces many extremal lattices of dimension $64$ [[Shi22]](#ref-Shi22).
- **2026.** Sakamoto constructs two extremal lattices of dimension $88$, with computer-assisted proofs (preprint) [[Sak26]](#ref-Sak26). In October, an extremal lattice of dimension $112$, $L_{112}$, is likewise announced with a computer-assisted proof (preprint) [[L112]](#ref-L112); the [page on $L_{112}$](../l112/) describes it.
